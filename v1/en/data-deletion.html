Data Deletion Instructions

At Newborn Tracker: Feeds & Sleep, we guarantee your right to be forgotten and the removal of your personal information in accordance with data protection regulations.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. What data is permanently deleted?

When the deletion is carried out, we will remove from our active servers (Firebase) the data of the whole family account, that is, of all its members:

  • User profiles (name and email address).
  • All baby records: breastfeeding, diapers, sleep, weight, height, solids, milestones, and medical data.
  • To-do lists and synchronized calendar events.
  • Feedback messages linked to your account, personalized settings, and preferences.

The app does not keep on our servers the photos used in the "Who does baby look like?" tool: they are processed on your device and not retained, so there is nothing to delete in that regard.

Note on Premium Subscriptions: Deleting your account within the app DOES NOT automatically cancel your active subscription on Google Play. Since payments are managed by Google, you must cancel your subscription through the Play Store to avoid future charges.

2. Data managed by third parties

For service management purposes, certain technical data is handled as follows after deletion:

  • RevenueCat: Transaction history is kept in an anonymized form for legal and accounting compliance but is decoupled from your personal identity.
  • Firebase Analytics: Previous usage data remains in aggregated statistical reports that do not allow for individual user identification.

3. Deletion Procedure

A. From the app (recommended)

Open the side menu and tap "Delete my account". Your request is registered and the app shows the notice "Account deletion scheduled". The data will be deleted within a maximum of 30 calendar days from the request. Until then you can tap "Cancel deletion" and the account will keep working normally. Once carried out, the deletion is irreversible.

B. Manual Request

If you do not have access to the app, send an email from the address associated with your account to:

wolfappsdevelopment@gmail.com

Subject: "Data Deletion Request". We will process the manual removal from all associated services within a maximum period of 30 calendar days.

4. Retention due to Inactivity

If the account shows no activity for a period of 24 months, the system may proceed with the automatic deletion of data to ensure the security of old information.
