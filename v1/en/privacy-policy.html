Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

At Newborn Tracker: Feeds & Sleep, we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information, including data related to premium subscriptions and usage analytics.

1. Information We Collect

  • User and Baby Data: Name, email, date and place of birth, gender, and health/activity logs (feeds, diapers, sleep, growth, solids, and milestones).
  • Account Data: User ID and, depending on the method you choose, your Google account or a guest account. The data of a family account is shared with the people you invite to it.
  • Feedback Messages: The text you send us from the app, together with your user ID and the date.
  • Subscription Data: Subscription status, purchase history, and an anonymous user ID to manage premium access.
  • Technical Data: Device identifiers, hardware model, operating system version, and the source of the installation (Google Play Install Referrer).

2. Subscriptions and Payments

The app offers subscription plans (monthly, yearly, or as available) for Premium features. To manage this, we use external providers:

Google Play Console (Google LLC): All payments are processed through the Google Play platform. We do not store or have access to your credit card data or sensitive banking information.

RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.): We use this tool to manage and validate your subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and purchase receipt details to ensure premium features are active on your device. View their policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Data Analytics and Security (Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to understand how users interact with the app and to improve it. This data is collected in an aggregated and pseudonymous way. We also use Firebase App Check (Play Integrity) to verify that requests to the server come from the original app.

  • Purpose: To measure app performance, identify crashes, analyze which features are most used, and protect the service from misuse.
  • Data Collected: Number of users and sessions, session duration, device models, and approximate geographic location (country/city).
  • If you wish to disable this collection, you can do so in your mobile device's privacy settings.

4. Camera, Photos and Notifications

  • "Who does baby look like?" tool: The app asks for camera access only for this tool; you can also choose photos from your gallery. Photos are analyzed on your own device with a face detection model included in the app (ML Kit). They are not sent to our servers or to third parties, and they are not stored: neither the photos nor the face measurements are kept when you close the tool.
  • Image to share: The result is generated on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it with another app. That image includes the portraits used, so share it only with whom you wish.
  • Notifications and alarms: The app may show reminders on your device. They are optional and you can turn them off in the system settings.

5. What Data We Store

To provide a personalized experience, we store the following data on our servers (Firebase):

  • Account information: Name, email address, and user ID.
  • Baby information: Name, date and place of birth, gender, and tracking data (feeding, sleep, etc.).
  • Medical data and calendar: Medical records and events related to the baby.
  • Feedback messages you send.
  • Subscription status: Technical records to validate your access to the premium program.

The app does not store photographs of the baby or the parents on our servers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as your account is active or as necessary to provide services and manage active subscriptions. If the account remains inactive for more than 24 months, we reserve the right to delete the associated data automatically, unless there are legal retention obligations (such as financial transaction records).

7. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and associated data:

A. From the app

Open the side menu and tap "Delete my account". The request is registered and the deletion is carried out within a maximum of 30 calendar days; until then you can cancel it from the app itself. The deletion affects the whole family account and the data of all its members. Note: Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription; this must be done in the Play Store settings.

B. By email

Send an email to wolfappsdevelopment@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". See our data deletion instructions page for details.

8. Data Protection

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access. Payment data is protected by Google Play's industry-standard encryption.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your personal data. As an app with subscription services, you are also entitled to receive a history of your transactions through Google's platforms.

10. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services or for legal reasons. We will notify you of any substantial change through the app.
